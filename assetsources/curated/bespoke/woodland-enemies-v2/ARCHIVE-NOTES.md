# Woodland Enemies V2 Archive Notes

Current enemy sprite authority lives in `docs/enemy-sprite-style-bible.md` and
the walk-only enemy asset pipeline. Enemy runtime sheets should use authored
`walk_up`, `walk_right`, `walk_down`, and `walk_left` rows unless a future
bible/pipeline explicitly changes that contract.

Attack GIFs and attack folders under this tree, including older grey wolf and
goblin review artifacts, are historical review material only. They are not
current enemy walk-sheet requirements and should not be used as art-direction
authority for new enemy sprites.
