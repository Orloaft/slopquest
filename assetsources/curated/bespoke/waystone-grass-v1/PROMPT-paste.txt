Top-down 2D RPG ground-texture tile sheet, hand-painted "Tibia / classic-MMO" style. A single
image laid out as a 4-columns × 2-rows grid of 8 separate 32×32 pixel ground tiles. Put an 8-pixel
PURE MAGENTA (#FF00FF) separator strip BETWEEN the columns and between the two rows — magenta gutters
between tiles ONLY, with NO magenta border around the outside. The first tile is flush to the
top-left corner. Final image size exactly 152 × 72 pixels (no upscaling, crisp pixels).

All 8 tiles are DARK, MUTED OLIVE-GREEN grass — earthy and slightly desaturated, NOT bright, NOT
lime, NOT neon. Target color: dominant midtone around #4F4B1A (dark khaki-olive green), shadow
clumps near #1E2410, blade highlights only up to a muted #6E7A30. Think shaded forest-town turf.

Hard requirement: every tile must TILE SEAMLESSLY when repeated (left edge wraps to right, top wraps
to bottom), AND all 8 tiles must share the same grass tone at their edges so any tile can sit beside
any other with no seam. Keep all flowers / patches / specks in the tile INTERIOR, never touching an
edge. Keep accents small and subtle — these repeat across a whole map.
